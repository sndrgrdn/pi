---
name: one-concept-one-home
description: Find a single concept scattered across modules instead of owned by one home.
severity-default: high
---

**One concept, one home** — a concept belongs in one module; scattered, it changes in many places for one reason and hides nothing anywhere. To record or change concept X once, list every module that must know — that list is the scatter, and ambient state (`Current.*`, globals) threaded to reach a site is a missing seam, not a home. **Presumptive blocker.** Evidence: the named concept, the module that should be its one home, and every scattered site (file:line).
